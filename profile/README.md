## Create Music Videos From Your Songs With HeyBop.ai

[HeyBop.ai](https://heybop.ai/) is an AI music video generator for artists, musicians, and music creators. Turn uploaded or AI-generated songs into original music videos with scenes edited to the beat. Choose a visual style, shape your story, and refine individual shots before exporting for social media or YouTube.

### Song to Video

- Upload your own track or create a song using Music Lab.
- Generate scenes around your music with beat-synced editing.
- Choose visual styles, describe scenes, and add reference photos.
- Adjust camera directions and regenerate individual shots.

### Flexible Video Exports

Export videos in MP4 format with vertical, landscape, square, and portrait layouts for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and YouTube.

### Music and Voice Creation

- **Music Lab:** Generate songs from prompts or lyrics.
- **Voice Lab:** Create voice-overs for creative projects.
- **Scene Control:** Refine individual shots without regenerating the entire video.
- **Cost Visibility:** Review generation costs before confirming each step.
